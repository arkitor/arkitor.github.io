Material advantages

Performance at a glance

Arkitor systems are developed around durable mineral construction, moisture management, reinforced strength and long-term serviceability. Performance must always be confirmed for the specific product and complete assembly.

Fire resistance
Mineral-based construction supporting fire-conscious design.
Moisture durability
Products developed for stable performance in moisture-exposed applications.
Reinforced strength
Fiberglass-mesh reinforcement used in applicable panel systems.
System integration
Panels, finishes, trims and prefabricated concepts coordinated as building systems.
