What is ArkiCore?

Arkitor’s interior mineral building system for wall, ceiling and prefabricated construction applications.

Can ArkiCore be used in modular construction?

Yes. It is developed to support panelized, modular and other off-site construction methods, subject to project-specific assembly review.

Can it be combined with ArkiTrim and ArkiDecor?

Yes. The systems are designed to coordinate for cleaner interior panel joints and architectural finishing.
