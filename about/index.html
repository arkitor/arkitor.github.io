Who we are

Building Systems Designed for Modern Construction

CCM Industries Inc. is a Canadian building materials company focused on developing advanced mineral-based building systems through the Arkitor brand.

Our focus

Building beyond individual boards

Arkitor connects practical construction requirements with advanced MgO, MgSO₄ and GFRC technologies. The goal is to develop coordinated systems that solve real building problems, from interior walls and floors to protective layers, architectural finishes and prefabricated construction.

We work with manufacturers, builders, distributors, modular companies, architects, engineers and other project partners to move from material selection into complete system thinking.

What we build

A connected product family

Each Arkitor system has a distinct role, but the systems can also be coordinated across the same project.

Mineral panel systems

ArkiCore, ArkiShield and ArkiFloor address interior, protective and flooring applications with mineral-based construction.

Architectural finishing

ArkiDecor and ArkiTrim coordinate mineral finish panels with clean aluminum joints, edges and architectural details.

Integrated systems

ArkiSIP and ArkiXT extend the product family into prefabricated construction and future exterior mineral composite development.

Canadian company

CCM Industries Inc.

Arkitor is a CCM Industries Inc. brand based in Richmond, British Columbia. CCM manages the Arkitor product family, market development, project coordination and North American customer relationships.

Our approach is to work with established manufacturing and industry partners rather than presenting every component as something CCM manufactures directly. That lets Arkitor focus on selecting, integrating and developing practical systems for North American construction.

Technical responsibility matters: product and assembly claims are matched to the specific configuration and supporting documentation. Where engineering, testing or authority approval is required, those requirements remain part of the complete project process.
