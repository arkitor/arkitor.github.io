Who we are

Building Systems Designed for Modern Construction

CCM Industries Inc. is a Canadian building materials company focused on developing advanced mineral-based building systems through the Arkitor brand.

Our Focus

Arkitor connects practical construction knowledge with advanced MgO, MgSO₄ and GFRC technologies to create useful building systems rather than isolated board products.

What We Build

Our product family covers interior systems, protective systems, flooring, decorative finishes, aluminum connection profiles, integrated prefabricated construction and future exterior composite platforms.

How We Work

Practical Product Selection

We help match products and systems to the actual application, project stage and construction method.

Technical Coordination

We provide current product information and coordinate project-specific questions with the appropriate technical documentation.

Product Development

Arkitor works with manufacturing and industry partners to develop new mineral-based building systems for North American construction.
