What is ArkiFloor?

Arkitor’s mineral subfloor panel system for approved residential, commercial, institutional, modular and prefabricated floor assemblies.

Is ArkiFloor intended for mass timber?

It can be reviewed as a mineral flooring platform for mass-timber and hybrid assemblies, subject to project-specific structural and system requirements.

What edge profiles are available?

Square-edge, shiplap and tongue-and-groove concepts can be reviewed. Confirm current production availability, tolerances and minimum quantities.

Can it be used with radiant heating?

Potential radiant applications require project-specific review of the complete assembly, operating conditions and current approved guidance.
