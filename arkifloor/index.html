Flooring Systems

ArkiFloor™

Mineral flooring systems for multifamily, modular and mass timber construction.

Stable mineral flooring platform

ArkiFloor uses high-density mineral board technology for subfloor and flooring applications where dimensional stability, moisture durability and non-combustible construction are valuable.

Subfloor applications
Available in structural and underlayment-oriented configurations depending on project requirements.
Modular and prefab
Well suited to factory-built construction and repeatable panelized floor assemblies.
Mass timber ready
Can be evaluated as a mineral layer within mass-timber and acoustic floor assemblies.

Typical Formats

18 mm structural formatShiplap and other edge profiles are available for selected subfloor applications.
Multiple panel sizesFormats vary by project, handling and installation requirements.
Assembly specificLoads, spans, fire and acoustic performance depend on the tested or engineered assembly.
