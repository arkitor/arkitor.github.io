System approach

Use the right layer for the right job

A complete building assembly may use more than one Arkitor system. The exact combination depends on the substrate, structure, climate, exposure, fire strategy, acoustic targets, finish requirements and construction method.

Interior layer
ArkiCore for walls and ceilings.
Protective layer
ArkiShield for demanding protective applications.
Floor layer
ArkiFloor for mineral subfloor concepts.
Finish layer
ArkiDecor and ArkiTrim for finished architectural surfaces.
Project process

Support before specification

1

Define the application

Building type, location, use, substrate, framing and expected exposure.

2

Identify performance goals

Fire, moisture, durability, structural, acoustic, thermal and finish requirements.

3

Match current documentation

Confirm the exact product, thickness, assembly and available technical support.

4

Coordinate samples & supply

Review samples, quantities, pricing, production, freight and project timing.
