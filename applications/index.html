Solutions

Mineral Building Systems for Modern Construction

Arkitor systems can be coordinated across multiple stages of construction, from interior boards and flooring to protective layers, decorative finishes and prefabricated building systems.

Interior Walls & Ceilings

ArkiCore systems for interior walls, ceilings, service areas and rated assemblies where the correct tested configuration is available.

Moisture & Protective Areas

ArkiShield configurations for roofing cover boards, soffits, wet conditions and demanding protective applications.

Flooring & Subfloor

ArkiFloor systems for modular, multifamily, mass timber and selected structural or underlayment applications.

Decorative Interiors

ArkiDecor and ArkiTrim combine mineral finish panels with coordinated aluminum profiles for clean finished surfaces.

Modular & Prefabricated

ArkiSIP coordinates insulated panel construction with Arkitor interior, exterior, flooring and finish layers on a project-specific basis.

Exterior Development

ArkiXT is the future mineral composite platform for advanced facade and exterior applications.

Project support before specification

Arkitor can help with product selection, current technical documentation, samples and project-specific coordination. Fire, structural, moisture, acoustic and envelope performance must be verified for the exact product and assembly being used.
