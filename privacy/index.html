Privacy at Arkitor

This notice explains how CCM Industries Inc. handles information submitted through arkitorbuild.com.

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Arkitor is a brand of CCM Industries Inc., based in Richmond, British Columbia, Canada. This website is an informational and project-support site for Arkitor products and systems.

Information you choose to provide

When you submit an inquiry, we may receive your name, email address, phone number, company, project location, product interests, project type, message, and other information you choose to include.

Referral and inquiry-source information

To help us understand and route inquiries, the contact form may also include the Arkitor page or product that led to the inquiry, the landing page, referring page, and standard campaign parameters such as UTM fields. These fields are used for normal business lead attribution and project follow-up.

How we use the information

CCM Industries Inc. may use inquiry information to respond to requests, provide pricing or availability information, coordinate samples, provide relevant technical information, discuss distribution or project opportunities, and maintain normal business records related to the inquiry.

Service providers

arkitorbuild.com is hosted through GitHub Pages. Product and marketing images are delivered from Shopify's content delivery network. Contact-form submissions are processed through Formspree and then routed to CCM Industries Inc. These service providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser information, timestamps, and server or delivery logs as part of operating their services.

Cookies and advertising

At the time of this notice, arkitorbuild.com does not intentionally run an advertising pixel or a separate visitor-analytics platform. Standard hosting and CDN logs may still be generated by the service providers used to operate the site.

Retention and disclosure

We retain inquiry and project information for as long as reasonably needed for customer service, project follow-up, business records, legal obligations, or legitimate operational requirements. Information may be shared with service providers or project partners when reasonably necessary to respond to the request, subject to applicable requirements.

Your choices

You may contact us to ask about personal information you submitted through this site or to request a correction or deletion where applicable. Please do not submit passwords, payment-card details, health information, government identification numbers, or other sensitive personal information through the inquiry form.
