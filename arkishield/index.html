1

Exposure layer

3

Transitions

Fasteners, trims, joints, edge details and project-specific interfaces.

4

What is ArkiShield?

Arkitor’s protective mineral building system for roofing, subfloor, soffit, wet-area, modular and other demanding applications.

Can ArkiShield be used outside?

It can be reviewed for coordinated exterior assemblies, but final use must be supported by the applicable product data, envelope design, climate exposure and project-specific requirements.

Can it integrate with other Arkitor systems?

Yes. It can coordinate with ArkiCore, ArkiFloor, ArkiTrim and developing Arkitor building systems.
