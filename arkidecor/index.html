Decorative Finishes

ArkiDecor™

Decorative mineral finish systems for premium interiors, architectural walls and protected surfaces.

Finished wall systems with a mineral substrate

ArkiDecor expands the Arkitor platform beyond unfinished board by combining mineral panels with factory-applied decorative surfaces for fast, consistent interior finishing.

2 mm Finish Panels

Thin decorative panels designed to be installed over a suitable substrate such as ArkiCore for a finished wall system.

9 mm Finish Panels

Thicker decorative panels that can be evaluated for direct-to-framing or other project-specific installation methods.

Pattern & Solid Colours

Available in pattern-and-colour and solid-colour finishes, with interlocking options for selected 9 mm configurations.

Designed to work with ArkiTrim

ArkiDecor can be paired with Arkitor aluminum trim profiles to create clean joints, protected edges and intentional architectural details.

Finish availability, colour, edge profile and installation method vary by product. Request current samples and technical information before specification.
