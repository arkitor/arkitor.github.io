Two formats

Choose the finish system for the wall build-up

2 mm series

ArkiDecor 2™

Thin decorative mineral finish panel designed for elegant interior renovations where minimal wall thickness and modern aesthetics are required.

Typical uses: feature walls, kitchen backsplashes, bathroom walls, elevator lobbies, retail interiors and residential renovations.

The 2 mm format normally requires a suitable substrate or backing system. Confirm the approved installation detail for the project.

9 mm series

ArkiDecor 9™

Architectural mineral protection panel for premium environments where appearance, durability and long-term performance are equally important.

Typical uses: commercial interiors, hospitality, institutional buildings, luxury residences, high-traffic public spaces and evaluated prefabricated applications.

The 9 mm format can be coordinated as a more robust panel solution with ArkiTrim profiles and approved attachment details.

What is the difference between ArkiDecor 2 and ArkiDecor 9?

ArkiDecor 2 is a thin finish panel intended for applications where a suitable substrate supports the finish. ArkiDecor 9 is a thicker architectural mineral panel intended for more robust finish-system applications.

Does ArkiDecor work with ArkiTrim?

Yes. ArkiTrim profile families are coordinated around the 2 mm and 9 mm ArkiDecor systems.

Can I request finish samples?

Yes. Contact Arkitor for current available patterns, colours, solid-colour options and matching trim samples.

Can it be used in modular construction?

ArkiDecor can be coordinated with modular and prefabricated interior systems, subject to the approved substrate, attachment and assembly requirements.
