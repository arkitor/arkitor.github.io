No. ArkiXT is currently an R&D platform rather than a commercially released product line.

What is Arkitor researching?

Areas include mineral composite materials, reinforcement, architectural surfaces, coordinated exterior systems and prefabricated envelope concepts.

Can project teams collaborate with Arkitor?

Yes. Teams can share exterior challenges, application requirements and development opportunities for discussion.

Are the images approved construction details?

No. They are concept visuals showing development directions and should not be used as construction documentation.
