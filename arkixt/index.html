ArkiXT™

Future exterior mineral composite platform for advanced facade systems and next-generation applications.

Next-generation exterior development

ArkiXT is Arkitor's development platform for future exterior mineral composite systems using advanced cementitious and GFRC-related technologies.

Facade Focused

Development is centered on exterior architectural and building-envelope applications.

Composite Platform

The platform is intended to explore advanced mineral composite construction beyond conventional board products.

Future Development

ArkiXT remains an R&D platform and should not be treated as a currently standardized product system.

ArkiXT is under development. Availability, technical data, approvals and applications will be published as the platform progresses.
