Technical review

What to include in your request

1

Product system

ArkiCore, ArkiShield, ArkiFloor, ArkiDecor, ArkiTrim, ArkiSIP or ArkiXT.

2

Application

Wall, ceiling, floor, roof, soffit, finish, modular assembly or other intended use.

3

Configuration

Thickness, size, edge, finish, substrate and any known assembly details.

4

Project requirements

Location, fire, structural, moisture, acoustic, thermal, code and schedule requirements.

Important technical note: For permit, code, structural, fire-rated, acoustic, thermal or exterior-envelope applications, final suitability depends on the exact product and complete assembly. Acceptance remains subject to the applicable design professional, authority having jurisdiction and project requirements. Current approved information should be reviewed before specification or ordering.
Why are complete technical packages provided by request?

Because product configurations and assemblies differ. Reviewing the request first reduces the risk of applying an unrelated test report or outdated document to the wrong project condition.

Does a fire test for one assembly apply to every Arkitor board?

No. Fire-resistance ratings apply to the specific complete tested assembly and configuration identified in the applicable documentation.

Can I request samples with the technical information?

Yes. Include the product system, format and project type so Arkitor can coordinate the most relevant sample package.

Can architects, engineers and modular manufacturers request project-specific information?

Yes. Project teams can provide drawings, assembly concepts and performance requirements for review and coordination.
