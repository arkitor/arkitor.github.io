Technical guidance: ArkiTrim profile selection depends on panel thickness, substrate, finish intent, movement, attachment and exposure. Confirm current profile dimensions, aluminum specification, coating, compatibility, fasteners and installation details before construction documentation or ordering.
Is ArkiTrim only for ArkiDecor?

The current profile families are primarily coordinated around ArkiDecor 2 mm and 9 mm systems. Other integrations should be reviewed project by project.

What profiles are available?

Joint, internal and external corner, end/termination and transition or reveal concepts are available. Current profile availability should be confirmed before specification.

Can the profiles be used in prefabricated construction?

Yes, the system is intended to support repeatable detailing for modular and panelized interior assemblies.

Can I request physical trim samples?

Yes. Contact Arkitor and identify whether you are evaluating the 2 mm or 9 mm ArkiDecor system.
